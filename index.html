What Lenders Look At

  • Credit score — every offer card shows the bank's published minimum.
  • Stable income — we size EMIs at 50% of net monthly income as a rule of thumb.
  • Low existing obligations — current EMIs are deducted before we size your request.
  • Employment type — some offers are published for salaried applicants only.
